FAQ

Quick answers for Australian buyers.

Can you make labels to the latest SAA requirements?

Yes. We build kits around customer supplied schedules and the latest SAA labelling support documents for Australian solar projects.

Can you copy our existing kit or competitor SKU?

Yes. Send a photo, PDF, artwork file or label list. We can match the kit count, wording, size, material and packaging style.

Do you supply both PV and Battery/BESS label kits?

Yes. We supply grid connect PV kits, battery/BESS kits, AC solar labels, plaques and inverter-specific custom label packs.

Can you add our logo and pack under our brand?

Yes. OEM labels, branded bags, customer SKU stickers and custom packing lists can be arranged for wholesalers and repeat buyers.

What information do you need for a quote?

Send label artwork or samples, quantity, material preference, kit count, packing style and destination. We will reply with pricing and lead time.

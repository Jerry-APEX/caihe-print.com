Frequently Asked Questions

Can you prevent paper cracking on fold lines?
Yes. For paper thicker than 128gsm or with dark background printing, we perform a Creasing process before folding to ensure a smooth, crack-free edge.
Do you check my design file?
Yes. Our prepress engineers check every file for bleed, resolution, and Folding Logic (page order) to ensure the final product is correct.
What is the MOQ?
For digital printing (small booklets), MOQ is 100 pcs. For offset printing (mass production), we recommend 1,000+ pcs for the best unit price.
What file format do you need?
We prefer PDF (CMYK) with 3mm bleed. If you have source files (AI/InDesign), please outline fonts to avoid missing text issues.

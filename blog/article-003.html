BOPP vs. Paper Labels: The Ultimate Comparison

Choosing the wrong material is the #1 reason for label failure. We often see paper labels wrinkling in the fridge or ink running when wet. To avoid this, you need to understand the difference between BOPP (Plastic) and Paper.

1. What is BOPP? (The Waterproof King)

BOPP (Biaxially Oriented Polypropylene) is a plastic film. It is the gold standard for durability.

  • Waterproof & Oil-proof: Perfect for shampoo, essential oils, and beverages.
  • Tear-resistant: You cannot tear it by hand.
  • Flexibility: Fits curved bottles without peeling.

2. What is Coated Paper? (The Eco Choice)

Paper labels are made from wood pulp. They are cheaper and offer a classic, natural texture.

  • Cost-effective: Great for shipping labels and dry food packaging (tea, coffee, biscuits).
  • Texture: Can be written on with a pen.
  • Not Waterproof: Will soak up water and wrinkle if not laminated.

Quick Comparison Table

Feature White BOPP Coated Paper

💡 Pro Tip:

If your product goes into a Fridge or Bathroom, always choose BOPP. If it stays on a Dry Shelf (like a cardboard box), Paper is enough.

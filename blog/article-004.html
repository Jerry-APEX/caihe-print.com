Die Cut vs. Kiss Cut Stickers: What is the Difference?

When you order custom stickers for your brand, you will face two main options: Die Cut and Kiss Cut. Once peeled and applied to a surface, they look exactly the same. However, the way they are presented before peeling is very different.

1. What are Die Cut Stickers?

Die Cut stickers are cut straight through both the vinyl layer and the paper backing. The final sticker matches the exact shape of your design (e.g., a star shape, a logo outline).

  • Presentation: Looks very premium. The custom shape makes the sticker itself stand out.
  • Best For: Handouts at events, retail swag, inclusion in package orders.

2. What are Kiss Cut Stickers?

Kiss Cut stickers are only cut through the vinyl layer, leaving the paper backing intact. The backing is usually a square or rectangle that is larger than the sticker itself.

  • Protection: The extra backing protects the delicate edges of your sticker during shipping.
  • Peeling: Easier to peel because you can bend the backing border to pop the sticker edge up.
  • Best For: Bulk stacks, fast application lines, intricate designs.

Comparison Table

Feature Die Cut Kiss Cut
Backing Shape Custom (Matches Design) Square / Rectangle
Presentation Premium (Wow Factor) Standard
Peel Speed Medium Fast (Easy Peel)
Edge Protection Medium High

💡 Pro Tip:

Selling individual stickers? Choose Die Cut for the best visual appeal.
Using stickers for packaging? Choose Kiss Cut (or Roll Labels) for faster peeling efficiency.

Roll Labels vs. Sheet Labels: Which Format is Right for You?

When ordering custom stickers, one of the first questions we ask is: "Do you need rolls or sheets?" The answer depends entirely on how you plan to apply them.

1. Roll Labels (Best for Machinery)

Roll labels are spun around a cardboard core (usually 3"). This format is essential if you are using an Automatic Labeling Machine.

  • Pros: Faster application, cheaper unit price for bulk orders (500+).
  • Cons: Harder to distribute individually.

2. Sheet Labels (Best for Hand Application)

Sheet labels usually come on A4 or letter-sized paper. They are perfect for small businesses applying labels by hand or for promotional handouts.

Conclusion

If you have a labeling machine, you MUST choose Rolls. If you are just starting out, Sheets might be more manageable.

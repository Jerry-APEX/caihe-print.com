5 Expert Tips to Fix Bubble Issues in Automatic Labeling

Bubbles under transparent labels are the nightmare of every production manager. Especially on clear glass bottles, they make the product look cheap. Here is how to fix them using professional adjustments.

1. Check Bottle Cleanliness

Dust and Oil are the enemies of adhesive. Even a fingerprint can cause a bubble. Ensure your bottles are air-blown or wiped before entering the labeling conveyor.

2. Adjust the Peel Plate Angle

The "Peel Plate" is the sharp metal edge where the label separates from the liner. If this angle is too loose, the label "floats" before hitting the bottle, trapping air. Adjust it to be as sharp and close to the bottle as possible.

3. Increase Roller Pressure

For round bottle machines (like our MT-50), the sponge roller must apply firm, even pressure immediately as the label touches the glass. If your sponge is old or too soft, replace it.

4. Match Label Material Stiffness

If your label is too soft (e.g., thin PE), it wrinkles easily. For high-speed automatic labeling, we recommend using BOPP or PET face stock with a PET liner (not paper liner) for better tension control.

Going Green: How to Switch to Sustainable Packaging

Sustainability is no longer just a trend; it's a requirement. With new EU and US regulations on plastic waste, brands must adapt. But going green doesn't mean your packaging has to look "boring."

1. Why Choose FSC® Certified Paper?

FSC (Forest Stewardship Council) certification ensures that the paper comes from responsibly managed forests. It provides a "Chain of Custody" from the tree to your final label.

  • Compliance: Meets strict global export standards.
  • Trust: You can print the FSC logo on your packaging to show consumers you care.
  • Quality: Same premium texture as standard art paper.

2. The Magic of Soy Ink

Traditional petroleum-based inks release high VOCs (Volatile Organic Compounds). At Caihe Tech, we use Soy-based Inks.

Soy ink is biodegradable and makes paper recycling easier. Surprisingly, it also produces more vibrant colors than oil-based ink because soybean oil is clearer.

3. Recyclable Material Choices

If you want a 100% eco-friendly product, avoid mixing materials (e.g., plastic label on a paper box). Instead, choose:

  • Brown Kraft Paper: Natural, organic look for handmade products.
  • FSC Art Paper: Clean white look for pharmaceutical/cosmetic boxes.
  • Wash-off Adhesive: Allows labels to be easily removed during the bottle recycling process.

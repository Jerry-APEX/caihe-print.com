Why Do Labels Peel Off? Top 3 Reasons & Fixes

There is nothing worse than seeing your beautiful product on a shelf with the label peeling off. It damages your brand image instantly. But don't worry, 90% of label failures are caused by these 3 simple reasons.

1. Wrong Adhesive for the Temperature

The Problem: You used a standard permanent adhesive, but your product is stored in a fridge or freezer.

The Fix: Standard glue crystallizes and loses stickiness below 0°C. You MUST use "Freezer Grade" Hot Melt Adhesive. It stays tacky even at -40°C.

2. The "Memory Effect" on Small Bottles

The Problem: You are applying a stiff label (like thick paper) onto a very small round bottle (e.g., 10ml vial).

The Fix: The label wants to return to its flat shape (memory effect), causing the edges to lift ("flagging"). Use a thinner material like 50 micron BOPP or PE which is flexible and wraps tightly.

3. Surface Contamination

The Problem: Dust, oil, or moisture on the bottle before labeling.

The Fix: If you are filling oil or shampoo, ensure no residue is on the outside. Even a fingerprint can cause lifting. Using an Automatic Labeling Machine with a wiping station solves this.

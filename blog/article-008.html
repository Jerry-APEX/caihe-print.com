Variable Data Printing: How to Give Every Label a Unique Identity?

Imagine printing 10,000 labels, and every single one has a different QR code, serial number, or customer name. This used to be impossible (or extremely expensive) with traditional printing plates. But with HP Indigo Digital Printing, it's standard procedure.

This technology is called Variable Data Printing (VDP), and it is changing the packaging game for B2B brands.

1. Anti-Counterfeiting & Traceability

For pharmaceutical, electronic, or high-value cosmetic brands, fake products are a huge risk. VDP solves this by assigning a Unique ID to every unit.

  • Serial Numbers: Print "No. 0001" to "No. 5000" sequentially.
  • Dynamic QR Codes: Consumers can scan the code to verify authenticity on your website.
  • Invisible Ink: We can even print invisible UV codes that only show under blacklight.

2. Marketing Campaigns (Scan to Win)

Want to run a "Scan to Win" lottery? You need every QR code to link to a unique prize URL. We can feed your Excel list of 10,000 unique URLs directly into our printing press. The machine prints them on the fly without slowing down.

3. How It Works (The Process)

It's simpler than you think:

  1. Design: Create your static artwork (logo, background) in Adobe Illustrator.
  2. Data: Prepare an Excel file (.csv) with your variable data (codes, names, numbers).
  3. Merge: Our prepress team merges the two using HP SmartStream software.
  4. Print: The HP Indigo press prints each frame with new data instantly.

Frequently Asked Questions

Die Cut vs Kiss Cut: Which is better?
Die Cut looks cleaner as a handout (cut to shape). Kiss Cut is easier to peel because of the extra backing border. Both stick the same!
Are your stickers waterproof?
Yes! All our vinyl stickers come with a UV Lamination layer. They are waterproof, scratch-resistant, and dishwasher safe for 3-5 years.
What is the Minimum Order Quantity?
We have No MOQ for standard vinyl stickers. You can order 50 pcs for a test run or 10,000 pcs for a campaign.
What file format do you need?
We prefer vector files like AI, PDF, or EPS for the best print quality. High-resolution PNG (300dpi) works too. We provide free cut-line proofs.

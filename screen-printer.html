Frequently Asked Questions

What inks can I use?
The machine is designed for UV Inks which cure instantly. It also supports Solvent inks with an optional hot air dryer tunnel.
Can it print Braille?
Yes! By using a thick screen mesh and high-build varnish, you can easily print tactile Braille warning triangles for pharmaceutical labels.
How precise is the registration?
With the CCD Camera System, we achieve ±0.05mm accuracy, allowing you to overprint perfectly on pre-printed digital labels.
Is it easy to change screens?
Yes, the screen frame holder is designed for quick changeovers. An experienced operator can switch jobs in 15-20 minutes.

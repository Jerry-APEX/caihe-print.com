Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to buy dies?
No! This is a digital blade cutter. It cuts directly from your AI/PDF vector line. You save money on every new design.
What materials can it cut?
It handles almost all label materials including PP, PET, PVC, Art Paper, and Kraft Paper. Max thickness around 0.3mm.
Is it hard to operate?
Very easy. If you can use a printer, you can use this machine. We provide training videos and remote support. Setup takes < 5 mins.
How fast is it?
For simple shapes (squares/circles), it can reach 10-12 meters/min. For complex shapes, it slows down slightly to maintain precision.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I know if the roll fits my machine?
Most machines use a standard 76mm (3") core and max outer diameter of 300mm. We can also customize 40mm or 25mm cores for desktop printers.
Can you print variable data (QR codes)?
Yes! With our HP Indigo digital press, every single label in the roll can have a unique serial number or QR code without extra cost.
Are the labels waterproof?
Yes, our BOPP and Vinyl materials are 100% waterproof and oil-proof. For frozen food, we use a special "Freezer Grade" adhesive that sticks at -40°C.
What is the lead time?
Digital printing orders (under 10,000 pcs) usually ship in 3-4 working days after artwork approval. Offset orders take 5-7 days.
